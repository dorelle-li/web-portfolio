Dorelle Li

About

I’m Dorelle, a senior at Rhode Island School of Design studying Industrial Design.

I design products and systems that make an experience feel like a connection rather than a transaction. My work finds small points of disconnect, between people or between a person and a machine, and rebuilds them into interactions that hold.

Play is central to my practice. It creates the conditions for genuine curiosity, where people engage openly and arrive at reflection on their own terms. I use it to close the distance between users and subjects that can feel opaque, from emotional processing to childhood development to the technology we use but rarely understand.
